## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is CIMA Learn?

CIMA Learn is a professional education platform offering **Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)** training in **Arbitration and Mediation**. The platform provides:

- 📚 **Professional Programme**: Structured three-level pathway (Associate → Member → Fellow)
- 🎯 **Adjunct Courses**: Standalone courses on specific topics
- 🏆 **Professional Certifications**: Internationally recognized credentials
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly**: Works on any device, installable as an app
- 🌍 **Global Access**: Learn from anywhere, at your own pace

## 📱 Installing the Mobile App (PWA)

CIMA Learn can be installed as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** on your device - it works like a native app!

### On Desktop (Chrome, Edge):

1. Look for the **"Install CIMA Learn"** button in the header
2. Click it and confirm the installation
3. The app will appear on your desktop/start menu
4. Launch it anytime without opening a browser

### On iPhone/iPad (Safari):

1. Open Safari and navigate to CIMA Learn
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Name it "CIMA Learn" and tap **Add**
5. Find the app icon on your home screen

### On Android:

1. Open Chrome and visit CIMA Learn
2. Tap the **three dots** menu (⋮)
3. Select **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. The app will be added to your app drawer

**Benefits of Installing:**
- ✓ Faster loading
- ✓ Works offline for downloaded content
- ✓ Native app experience
- ✓ Easy access from home screen
- ✓ No browser tabs needed

## 🎯 First Login & Onboarding

### Your First Login

1. Go to the CIMA Learn website
2. Click **"Login"** in the top-right corner
3. Enter your **email** and **password**
4. Click **"Sign In"**

### Complete Your Profile (Onboarding)

After your first login, you'll be guided through an **onboarding process** to personalize your experience:

#### Step 1: Personal Information
- **Country**: Select your location
- **Phone Number**: For important notifications
- **Date of Birth**: Required for certificates
- **Profile Photo**: Upload a professional photo (optional but recommended)

#### Step 2: Professional Background
- **Professional Background**: Lawyer, Judge, Student, etc.
- **Highest Qualification**: Your education level
- **Years of Experience**: Your professional experience
- **Industry**: Legal, Business, Government, etc.
- **Current Role**: Your job title/role

#### Step 3: ADR Experience
- **Do you have ADR experience?**
  - **Yes**: You may qualify for advanced access (Member/Fellow level)
  - **No/Undecided**: You'll start at Associate level (recommended for beginners)

If you have experience, you'll be asked to provide:
- **Years of ADR Experience**
- **Professional Bio** (describing your experience)
- **Supporting Documents** (certificates, case records, etc.)

**💡 Pro Tip:** If you're unsure, start with Associate level. You can always apply for advancement later after demonstrating competency.

#### Step 4: Profile Review

- If you claim **advanced experience**, your profile will be reviewed by the admissions team (typically within 48 hours)
- You'll get **Associate access immediately** while waiting
- Once approved, you'll be upgraded to Member or Fellow level

## 🔍 Exploring Courses

### Main Navigation Areas:

1. **Home Page** (`/home`): Your personalized dashboard
2. **Browse Courses** (`/courses`): Full course catalog
3. **Learning Pathways** (`/programs`): Structured programs (Associate → Member → Fellow)

### Course Categories:

Courses are organized by:

- **Programme Type:**
  - 🎓 **Professional Programme**: Part of the structured pathway
  - 📚 **Adjunct Course**: Standalone courses

- **Level:**
  - 🟢 **Associate** (Part I): Entry-level, no prerequisites
  - 🟡 **Member** (Part II): Intermediate, requires Associate completion
  - 🔴 **Fellow** (Part III): Advanced, requires Member completion

- **Track:**
  - ⚖️ **Arbitration**: International arbitration, dispute resolution
  - 🤝 **Mediation**: Conflict resolution, negotiation

### Browsing Courses:

1. **Search Bar**: Type keywords (e.g., "maritime mediation")
2. **Filters**: 
   - Filter by Level (Associate, Member, Fellow)
   - Filter by Track (Arbitration, Mediation)
   - Filter by Category
   - Filter by Price Range
3. **Sort Options**: Newest, Price (Low to High), Popular

### Course Cards Show:

- ✓ Course thumbnail
- ✓ Course title and subtitle
- ✓ Level badge (Associate/Member/Fellow)
- ✓ Track badge (Arbitration/Mediation)
- ✓ Category (e.g., "MEDIATION")
- ✓ Price (in USD and GHS)
- ✓ Enrollment count
- ✓ Instructor name
- ✓ Eligibility status (Eligible, Locked, Enrolled)

### Eligibility:

Courses show different statuses:

- ✅ **Eligible**: You can enroll (prerequisites met)
- 🔒 **Locked**: Complete prerequisites first
- 📖 **Enrolled**: You're already enrolled
- ⭐ **Next Step**: Recommended next course in your pathway

## 💳 Payment Process

### Checkout Steps:

#### **Step 1: Review Your Order**

You'll see:
- Course thumbnail and details
- Price breakdown (course price, any discounts)
- Total amount in **GHS** (Ghanaian Cedis)

**Have an Access Code?**
- Enter your code in the "Access Code" field
- Click **"Apply"** to receive your discount
- The total will update automatically

**Company/Invoice?**
- Check **"Request company invoice"**
- Enter company name, email, and VAT ID
- You'll receive an invoice after payment

#### **Step 2: Choose Payment Method**

**Option A: Online Payment (Paystack)**
- Pay with Card, Mobile Money, or Bank Transfer
- Instant access after payment
- Most common method

**Option B: Company Invoice**
- For companies paying via bank transfer
- Manual verification required
- Access granted after payment confirmation

#### **Step 3: Complete Payment**

1. Click **"Proceed to Payment"**
2. Enter your card details (or choose Mobile Money)
3. Complete the payment securely via Paystack
4. You'll be redirected back to CIMA Learn

**🎉 Success!**
- You'll see a confirmation message
- Confetti animation celebrates your enrollment
- You'll receive a confirmation email
- **Click "Go to Course"** to start learning

## 🎯 Professional Pathways

### Understanding the Pathway System:

CIMA Learn offers **two professional tracks**:

#### **1. Arbitration Track** (ACIMArb → MCIMArb → FCIMArb)

**Part I - Associate Level:**
- Entry point for beginners
- Fundamental arbitration concepts
- No prerequisites required
- ~3-6 courses

**Part II - Member Level:**
- Intermediate level
- Must complete Associate courses first
- Practical skills development
- ~3-5 courses

**Part III - Fellow Level:**
- Advanced expert level
- Must complete Member courses first
- Complex dispute resolution
- ~2-4 courses

#### **2. Mediation Track** (ACIMed → MCIMed → FCIMed)

Same structure as Arbitration, focused on mediation and conflict resolution.

### Progression Requirements:

To advance from one level to the next:

1. ✓ **Complete all required courses** at your current level
2. ✓ **Pass all assessments** (if applicable)
3. ✓ **Earn certificates** for completed courses
4. ✓ **System automatically unlocks** next level courses

### Viewing Your Progress:

1. Go to **"Learning Pathways"** or **"Programs"**
2. Select your track (Arbitration or Mediation)
3. See visual pathway:
   - ✅ **Completed**: Green checkmarks
   - 🔄 **In Progress**: Yellow highlights
   - 🔒 **Locked**: Grayed out

## 🆘 Common Questions

### **Q: Can I enroll in multiple courses at once?**
**A:** Yes! Enroll in as many courses as you like. However, focus on completing one at a time for best results.

### **Q: How long do I have access to a course?**
**A:** Lifetime access! Once enrolled, you can revisit the course materials anytime.

### **Q: Can I get a refund?**
**A:** Refund policies vary. Check the Terms of Service or contact support before enrolling.

### **Q: Do certificates expire?**
**A:** No, your certificates are permanent. However, some professional certifications may require renewal for membership status.

### **Q: Can I download lessons?**
**A:** This depends on the course. Some allow downloads, others are streaming-only to protect content.

### **Q: What if I fail an assessment?**
**A:** Most assessments can be retaken. Check the specific course requirements.

### **Q: How do I change my track (Arbitration → Mediation)?**
**A:** You can pursue both tracks simultaneously! There's no restriction.

### **Q: Is there a mobile app?**
**A:** Yes! Install the PWA (Progressive Web App) following the instructions above. It works like a native app.

### **Q: Can companies enroll multiple employees?**
**A:** Yes! Contact support for company/group enrollment options and pricing.

### **Q: How are certificates verified?**
**A:** Each certificate has a unique ID. Employers can verify authenticity via the certificate verification page (if available).
